# Murmuration

Terms for the proposed Arma Reforger autonomous drone prototype.

## Language

**Reconnaissance drone**:
An autonomous aircraft that searches for and reports observed enemy players or vehicles.

**Scout point**:
The location a player selects on the map for the drones to investigate.

**Reconnaissance mission**:
A request to launch drones from a fixed point, scout a selected point, report sightings, and return.

**Swarm**:
The group of three autonomous reconnaissance drones assigned to the prototype mission.

**Last-seen marker**:
A map marker showing a target's most recently observed position rather than its continuously updated position.
